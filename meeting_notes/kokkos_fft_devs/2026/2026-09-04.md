## Callbacks
### Progress Since Last Meeting (August 21, 2026)

#### Completed
1. **Functor Strategy Prototype**
   - **Conclusion**: Functors **cannot bypass `MemcpyFromSymbol`**.
   - **Reason**: `operator()` is a **member function** → cannot be converted to a raw function pointer (required by C APIs like `*XtSetCallback`).

2. **`Callbackable` Trait**
   - **Status**: Implemented and tested.
   - **Files**:
     - `kokkos-fft/common/src/KokkosFFT_Traits.hpp` (trait + `Callbackable_v`).
     - `kokkos-fft/common/unit_test/Test_Traits.cpp` (tests for CUDA/HIP/Serial/OpenMP).
   - **Purpose**: Compile-time detection of callback support for `ExecutionSpace`.
   - **Test Results**:
     - ✅ CUDA: `CudaIsCallbackable` passes.
     - ✅ HIP: `HIPIsCallbackable` passes (tested on Adastra).
     - ✅ Serial/OpenMP: `SerialIsNotCallbackable`/`OpenMPIsNotCallbackable` pass.

3. **`MemcpyFromSymbol`**
   - **Design**: Recommended to be embedded inside a Callback wrapper class/function. (Same strategy as `KokkosFFT_*_types.hpp` wherein the HIP APIs are directly used when needed. General interfaces have not been introduced.)

1. Could not find a way to implement a Callback wrapper yet. Symbol not found?
